# Compensation and Benefits Guide

## Salary Bands and Review Cadence

Every role is mapped to a salary band based on level and location.
Compensation is formally reviewed once per year, in the March review cycle,
with adjustments effective April 1st. An employee promoted mid-year
receives an off-cycle compensation review within 30 days of the promotion
taking effect, rather than waiting for the next annual cycle.

## Equity and Vesting

New-hire equity grants vest over 4 years with a 1-year cliff: no shares vest
before the first anniversary of the grant date, after which 25% vests
immediately and the remainder vests monthly over the following 36 months.
Refresh equity grants, given at the discretion of a manager during the
annual review cycle, vest monthly over 4 years with no cliff.

## Retirement Matching

The company matches 401(k) contributions dollar-for-dollar up to 4% of an
employee's base salary. The match is deposited each pay period, not as a
single annual lump sum, and vests immediately — there is no vesting
schedule on the employer match itself.

## Health Insurance Enrollment

Health insurance elections are made during the annual open enrollment
period each November, with coverage effective January 1st of the following
year. New hires have 30 days from their start date to enroll outside the
normal open enrollment window; missing that 30-day window means waiting
until the next open enrollment period, except in the case of a qualifying
life event.

## Professional Development Budget

Each employee has an annual professional development budget of $1,500,
which can be used for courses, conference attendance, books, or
certification exam fees. Unused budget does not roll over to the following
year and cannot be exchanged for cash. Manager approval is required for any
single expense over $500 from this budget.

## Employee Referral Bonus

An employee who refers a candidate who is hired receives a referral bonus
of $3,000 for most roles, or $5,000 for hard-to-fill engineering and sales
leadership roles, paid out in two installments: half after the new hire's
first 30 days, and half after their first 6 months, contingent on the
referred employee still being employed at each payment date.

## Sabbatical Program

Employees become eligible for a 4-week paid sabbatical after 5 years of
continuous employment, and again every 5 years thereafter. Sabbatical time
is separate from and does not count against standard PTO balance, and must
be scheduled at least 60 days in advance with manager approval.
