# Customer Success Playbook

## Onboarding Timeline

New Professional and Enterprise plan customers are assigned a dedicated
Customer Success Manager (CSM) within 1 business day of signing. The
standard onboarding period is 30 days, during which the CSM runs a kickoff
call, a data migration session, and a final go-live review. Starter plan
customers do not receive a dedicated CSM but have access to self-serve
onboarding guides and email support.

## Data Migration

Customers migrating from a competing product may request a guided data
migration, available only on the Enterprise plan at no additional cost, or
on the Professional plan for a one-time fee. A guided migration is
completed within 10 business days of the customer providing a complete data
export; migrations that stall due to incomplete customer-provided data are
paused, not cancelled, and resume once the customer supplies the missing
data.

## Support Response SLAs

Enterprise plan customers are guaranteed a first response within 1 hour for
issues marked urgent, and 4 hours for standard issues, 24 hours a day, 7
days a week. Professional plan customers are guaranteed a first response
within 4 business hours for urgent issues and 1 business day for standard
issues. Starter plan customers receive best-effort support with no
guaranteed response time.

## Renewal and Notice Periods

Annual contracts renew automatically unless the customer provides written
notice of non-renewal at least 30 days before the renewal date. Enterprise
contracts longer than one year require 60 days' written notice instead.
A customer who cancels mid-term on a plan billed annually is not entitled
to a prorated refund for the remaining term, except where required by
local law.

## Health Score and Churn Risk

Each customer account is assigned a health score from 1 to 100, recalculated
weekly from product usage, support ticket volume, and NPS survey responses.
An account whose health score drops below 40 is automatically flagged to
the CSM team as at-risk, triggering a required check-in call within 5
business days. Accounts flagged as at-risk three consecutive weeks are
escalated to the VP of Customer Success.

## Training and Certification

Enterprise customers may enroll up to 5 administrators per year in the
company's free admin certification program, a self-paced course followed
by a proctored exam; a passing score is 80% or higher, and a failed exam may
be retaken once after a 7-day waiting period. Certification is valid for 18
months before requiring recertification.

## Quarterly Business Reviews

Enterprise customers receive a Quarterly Business Review (QBR) from their
CSM, covering usage trends, upcoming feature roadmap relevant to the
customer, and renewal planning. A QBR is scheduled at least 2 weeks in
advance and must be attended by at least one customer stakeholder above the
individual-contributor level for the review to count toward the customer's
QBR cadence; a review with no qualifying attendee is rescheduled, not
recorded as complete.

## Escalation to Product and Engineering

A support ticket may be escalated directly to the product team only if it
represents a confirmed bug affecting an Enterprise customer with no
workaround; escalated tickets receive a same-business-day acknowledgment
from a product manager and a target resolution or workaround within 5
business days.
