# Employee Handbook

## Paid Time Off

Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year,
credited at a rate of 1.25 days per completed month of employment. PTO does
not begin accruing until an employee has completed their 90-day probationary
period, though it accrues retroactively back to the hire date once that
period ends. Unused PTO carries over into the following year up to a maximum
of 5 days; any balance beyond that is forfeited on December 31st unless local
law requires otherwise.

To request PTO, submit a request through the HR portal at least 5 business
days in advance for absences of 3 days or fewer, and at least 3 weeks in
advance for absences longer than 3 days. Manager approval is required for all
PTO requests. Sick leave is tracked separately from PTO and does not require
advance notice.

## Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week without prior approval,
provided their team's core collaboration hours (10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in the
team's primary time zone) are maintained. Fully remote arrangements (5 days
per week) require written approval from both the employee's manager and HR,
and are reviewed annually.

Employees working remotely are responsible for maintaining a secure home
network, including a password-protected Wi-Fi connection and up-to-date
operating system security patches. Company laptops must never be left
unattended in a public space. Video calls involving confidential client
information should not be taken in shared or public workspaces.

## Expense Reimbursement

Business expenses are reimbursed through the expense portal within 30 days of
the expense being incurred; expenses submitted after 30 days may be denied at
manager discretion. Original itemized receipts are required for any single
expense over 25 dollars — a credit card statement alone is not sufficient
documentation.

Meal expenses while traveling for business are capped at 75 dollars per day,
inclusive of tips. Alcohol is reimbursable only when directly related to
client entertainment and must be itemized separately from the meal cost.
Personal entertainment, mini-bar charges, and in-flight Wi-Fi for personal
use are not reimbursable.

## Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to treat colleagues, clients, and partners with
respect and professionalism. Harassment, discrimination, or retaliation of
any kind will not be tolerated and should be reported immediately to HR or
through the anonymous ethics hotline. Reports are investigated within 10
business days, and retaliation against anyone who files a good-faith report
is grounds for immediate termination.

Employees must disclose any outside employment or business interests that
could reasonably be seen as a conflict of interest with the company's
business, using the conflict-of-interest disclosure form in the HR portal.
