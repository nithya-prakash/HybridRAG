# Attribution

The `.md` files in this folder are text extracts of English Wikipedia articles, fetched with
`fetch_wiki.py` on 2026-10-09 and truncated to 16,000 characters each. Wikipedia text is available
under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 license (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
Each file's first line is the article title; the full articles and their authors are listed on
en.wikipedia.org under that title (page history). The files are used only as test data for the
retrieval evaluation and are not covered by this repository's MIT license.
